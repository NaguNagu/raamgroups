# Website content plan

## Brand and audience

Sri Raam Rope Merchants is the storefront identity, with Raam Groups as the parent business. The audience includes farmers and cattle owners, households, local retailers and wholesale buyers. The voice is direct, warm and practical: useful product guidance, clear enquiries and a visible Salem address.

The visual system uses the supplied logo, deep forest green (#133a29 / #17462e), cream (#f8f7f2), muted green and a restrained gold (#f0c45c). Playfair Display provides the heritage tone on public headings; Inter keeps forms and admin tasks easy to scan. The supplied restaurant guide informs the system, not the site's business identity.

## Home

Headline: **Strong ropes. Stronger relationships.**

Supporting copy: Ropes, cattle essentials and traditional brassware. Serving farms, homes and businesses for generations.

Primary action: Explore our products. Secondary action: Talk to our team on WhatsApp.

Sections: business trust signals, four product families, everyday essentials, brief heritage story and contact guidance. The 50+ years claim is carried from the supplied poster and needs owner confirmation.

## About

Headline: **Built on trust. Bound by tradition.**

Describe the shop's role in Shevapet, its product breadth, retail and wholesale service, practical guidance and personal relationships. Avoid invented founding dates, certifications, production capabilities, performance promises or testimonials.

## Products

Headline: **Essentials you can rely on.**

Categories: Ropes; Cattle accessories; Brass & pooja; Cot tape rolls.

Each product needs: real name, application-focused description, material/size when confirmed, selling unit, price before GST, GST rate, HSN, stock, photo and publication status. Current example entries remain clearly indicative with prices on enquiry. Do not imply load ratings or safety suitability without product specifications.

## Contact

Headline: **Good conversations start here.**

Show store address, two phone numbers, email, WhatsApp, map directions and a short enquiry form. Do not invent opening hours; ask customers to call before travelling until the owner confirms hours.

## Order journey

Browse → product details → add to cart → adjust quantities → enter name/mobile → open prepared WhatsApp message → customer sends → company confirms stock, GST, delivery and price. The cart is an order request; no online payment or automatic order acceptance is claimed.

## Owner-maintained content

The owner can replace the hero banner and headline for occasions, edit product categories/prices/photos/stock, and update business details. Keep occasion artwork free of embedded small print because the hero is cropped responsively. New billing-only items stay hidden until reviewed for the public catalogue.

## Source boundaries

The supplied poster and logo are the actual content sources. The Google share and Justdial pages could not be retrieved during the build; no claim is presented as verified against those pages. The style guide is a design reference; its unrelated product names, restaurant modules and assistant imagery were not treated as user requirements.
